# Troubleshooting Guide

Common issues and their fixes. If none of these resolve the problem,
contact support with your order number and, where relevant, the device
model and operating system version.

## Device not recognised when connected

If a USB device is not detected, try a different port before assuming a
fault. On desktops, prefer the ports on the back of the machine, which are
wired directly to the motherboard.

Unpowered hubs frequently cannot supply enough current for multiple
devices at once. A USB-C Hub with several peripherals attached may drop
connections intermittently under load. Connecting the hub to a powered
source resolves most of these cases.

If the device works on another computer, the fault is with the host
machine's drivers rather than the hardware, and a warranty claim will be
declined on inspection.

## Wireless connection drops

Wireless peripherals operate on the 2.4 GHz band, which is shared with
most home Wi-Fi networks, cordless phones, and microwaves. Interference
usually presents as brief cursor stutter rather than a full disconnect.

Move the receiver to a front-facing port or use a short extension cable to
get it clear of metal enclosures. Replace the battery before assuming a
hardware fault: low battery causes intermittent drops long before the
device stops working entirely.

Pairing can be reset by holding the connect button for ten seconds until
the indicator flashes twice.

## Desk height controls unresponsive

A Standing Desk that will not move in either direction has usually
tripped its thermal cutout after too many consecutive adjustments. The
cutout resets on its own after 20 minutes. This is expected behaviour and
not a defect.

If the desk moves in one direction only, the control box has lost its
calibration. Hold the down button until the desk reaches its lowest
position, continue holding for five more seconds, then release. The desk
will complete a short reset cycle.

Uneven movement or a grinding sound indicates a mechanical fault and
should be raised as a warranty claim rather than adjusted further.

## Application will not sign in

Sign-in failures immediately after a password change usually mean the
session cache still holds the old credentials. Sign out fully on every
device, then sign in again.

Password reset links are valid for 30 minutes. Requesting several resets
in a row invalidates all earlier links, so use the most recent email.

If sign-in fails with a correct password and a valid link, check whether
the platform is inside its scheduled maintenance window before contacting
support.

## Order status has not changed

Order status updates when the carrier scans the parcel, which can lag
dispatch by up to 24 hours. A status that has not moved for more than two
business days past the expected delivery estimate should be reported so a
carrier trace can be opened.

Tracing takes three to five business days. Replacements are not dispatched
until the trace concludes, unless the order was sent by express shipping.
