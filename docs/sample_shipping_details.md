# Shipping and Delivery Details

Detailed delivery information. Headline delivery times are set out in the
Customer Support Policy; this document covers the surrounding process.

## Dispatch and cut-off times

Orders placed before 2:00 PM on a business day are dispatched the same
day. Orders after that cut-off, or on a weekend or public holiday, are
dispatched the next business day.

Delivery estimates begin at dispatch, not at the time the order is placed.
An order placed on Friday afternoon therefore ships Monday, and a standard
domestic estimate of 5-8 business days starts from that Monday.

Items shown as out of stock, such as the USB-C Hub, ship when replenished.
Backorders are dispatched automatically and are not charged again.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed at dispatch and appears in the account portal
under Orders. Tracking can take several hours to become active while the
carrier registers the parcel.

Split shipments generate one tracking number per parcel. Orders containing
both a furniture item and small electronics are commonly split, because
furniture ships on a pallet carrier and smaller items go by parcel
network.

## Delivery attempts

Carriers make two delivery attempts on consecutive business days. After a
second failed attempt the parcel is held at a local depot for ten calendar
days before being returned to us.

Parcels returned as undelivered are refunded minus the original outbound
shipping cost. Reshipping requires a new order.

Furniture deliveries are booked with a scheduled window and require
someone present to sign. Missed furniture appointments incur a
redelivery fee of $35.

## International orders

International shipping is available to select countries and takes 10-15
business days. The destination list is shown at checkout; countries not
listed cannot be shipped to, including by freight forwarder.

Import duties, taxes, and customs handling fees are set by the destination
country and are payable by the recipient. These are not included in the
price shown at checkout and are not refundable if the order is later
returned.

Customs inspections can add a week or more and are outside the estimates
above. Parcels held in customs are not eligible for a carrier trace until
released.

## Address changes

Addresses can be edited in the account portal until the order enters
dispatch. After dispatch, the address is fixed with the carrier and cannot
be changed by support.

Incorrect addresses that result in a failed delivery follow the
undelivered parcel process above. We cannot recover parcels delivered
successfully to an address the customer entered incorrectly.
