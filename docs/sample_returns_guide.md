# Returns and Exchanges Guide

This guide explains how to return or exchange an Acme Corp order. It
expands on the refund terms in the Customer Support Policy; where the two
documents overlap, the Customer Support Policy is authoritative.

## Starting a return

Returns begin in the account portal under Orders > Return an Item. Select
the order, choose the items being returned, and pick a reason from the
list. A prepaid return label is generated immediately for domestic orders
and emailed to the address on the account.

Returns must be started within the 30-day refund window that begins on the
date of purchase. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.
Orders started after day 30 are declined automatically by the portal and
cannot be reopened by support.

## Return shipping costs

Domestic return shipping is free when the return is caused by a defect,
damage in transit, or a fulfilment error on our side. For change-of-mind
returns, a flat return shipping fee of $6.95 is deducted from the refund.

International returns are the customer's responsibility, and duties or
customs charges already paid are not refundable.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order. The replacement item
ships once the returned item is scanned by the carrier, not once it
arrives at the warehouse, which usually saves three to five days. If the
replacement costs more than the original item, the difference is charged
to the original payment method.

Exchanges are limited to one per order line. A second exchange request on
the same line is handled as a standard return and refund.

## Condition assessment

Returned items are inspected on arrival. Items showing use beyond
reasonable inspection may receive a partial refund, reduced by up to 40%
depending on condition. The inspection result and any deduction are
emailed before the refund is issued, and can be disputed within 14 days by
replying to that email.

Missing accessories, cables, or manuals reduce the refund by the
replacement cost of the missing parts.

## Refund timing

Once a return passes inspection, refunds are processed within 5-7 business
days to the original payment method. Card issuers may take an additional
two to five business days to post the credit, which is outside our
control.

Refunds cannot be redirected to a different card or account. If the
original payment method is closed, the refund is issued as account credit
instead.

## Items that cannot be returned

Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded. Gift cards, redeemed
promotional credit, and clearance items marked "final sale" are also
non-returnable. Bulk orders above 25 units require a returns authorization
from the enterprise support team before shipping anything back.
