# Billing and Subscriptions FAQ

## When am I charged for an order?

Card payments are authorised when the order is placed and captured at
dispatch. An authorisation is not a charge; it reserves the amount against
the available balance. If an order is cancelled before dispatch, the
authorisation is released and no charge appears, though the reserved
amount can take up to five business days to clear back.

## Why do I see two charges for one order?

Split shipments are captured separately, so an order dispatched in two
parcels produces two charges that together match the order total. This is
most common when an order mixes furniture and electronics.

A duplicate charge for the identical amount at the identical time is
usually a pending authorisation shown alongside the settled charge, and
resolves on its own.

## How does subscription billing work?

Subscriptions bill on the same calendar day each month, or on the last day
of the month for start dates after the 28th. The billing date is set when
the subscription starts and does not move.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated against the remainder of
the current cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next cycle,
so the current cycle continues at the existing tier.

Cancelling stops future billing but does not refund the current cycle. See
the Customer Support Policy for the cancellation terms.

## Can I get an invoice or receipt?

Receipts are emailed automatically at capture. Formal invoices, including
VAT or GST registration numbers, are available in the account portal under
Billing > Documents and can be regenerated at any time.

Invoices can be issued to a company name and billing address different
from the delivery address. Set these under Billing > Company Details
before the order is placed; invoices already issued cannot be edited.

## What happens if a payment fails?

Failed subscription payments are retried three times over seven days.
Notification emails are sent after each failure. If all three retries
fail, the subscription is suspended rather than cancelled, and access is
restored as soon as a successful payment is taken.

Suspended subscriptions are cancelled automatically after 30 days, at
which point the original pricing may no longer be available.

## Which payment methods work for subscriptions?

Visa, Mastercard, and American Express can be used for subscriptions.
PayPal is supported for one-off orders only, because our subscription
processor cannot take recurring PayPal authorisations.

Bank transfers are available to enterprise accounts and are billed
annually in advance rather than monthly.

## Do prices include tax?

Prices shown in the product catalogue, such as $24.99 for the Wireless
Mouse or $349.00 for the Standing Desk, exclude tax. Applicable sales tax,
VAT, or GST is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address and
shown before payment is confirmed.
