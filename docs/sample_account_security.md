# Account and Security Guide

How to manage an Acme Corp account, and what we do with the data attached
to it.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is available under Settings > Security and is
strongly recommended. Both authenticator apps (TOTP) and SMS codes are
supported; authenticator apps are more reliable because they work without
mobile reception.

Enabling two-factor authentication generates ten single-use recovery
codes. Each code works once. Store them outside the account, since they
are the only way back in if the second factor is lost.

Support cannot disable two-factor authentication on request. This is
deliberate: an account takeover would otherwise only need access to the
registered email address. Accounts locked out without recovery codes go
through identity verification, which takes five to seven business days.

## Sessions and devices

Active sessions are listed under Settings > Security > Devices, showing
the approximate location and last activity time for each. Any session can
be revoked individually, or all at once with "Sign out everywhere".

Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity. Changing the account password
revokes every other session immediately.

## Email address changes

Changing the registered email requires confirmation from both the old and
new addresses. The confirmation link sent to the old address is valid for
24 hours; if it expires, the change is discarded and must be restarted.

If the old address is no longer accessible, the change requires identity
verification through support.

## Data retention

Order history is retained for seven years to meet tax and accounting
obligations. This retention applies even after account closure and cannot
be shortened by request.

Support conversations are retained for two years. Marketing preferences
and analytics data are deleted within 30 days of account closure.

## Account closure

Accounts are closed from Settings > Account > Close Account. Closure is
irreversible and takes effect after a 14-day grace period, during which
signing in cancels the closure.

Closing an account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel the
subscription first, or it continues billing until the end of the current
cycle. Any open orders continue to delivery, and the returns window on
them remains valid.

## Suspicious activity

Sign-in attempts from an unrecognised device trigger a notification email
to the registered address. If a sign-in was not initiated by the account
holder, change the password immediately and use "Sign out everywhere".

We never ask for a password, recovery code, or full card number by email
or phone. Messages requesting these are not from us, regardless of how the
sender address appears.
